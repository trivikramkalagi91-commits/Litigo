How It Works

Three steps.
Total control.

Fall 2026 / YC × Moss
01

Install & set your rules

Add the extension, click the icon, and define your rules once. "Keep answers under 50 words." "Never mention competitors." "Always use bullet points."

02

Chat normally

Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — any LLM. Type your questions like you always do. Letigo works invisibly in the background.

03

See violations caught live

Watch as the AI types. Rule-breaking text gets red strikethrough in real-time. A compliance score tells you exactly how trustworthy each response is.

Technology

Powered by Moss. Built for trust.

Letigo uses Moss — a sub-10ms semantic search engine running entirely in your browser as WebAssembly.

10ms

Sub-10ms semantic validation

Fast enough to validate streaming output in real-time. Zero perceptible lag to the user.

20kB

Tiny WASM runtime

The entire Moss engine fits in under 20 kilobytes. Loads instantly, no bloat, no heavy dependencies.

∞

Privacy by design

Everything runs locally. No telemetry. No cloud calls. Your rules and conversations stay yours.

How Data Flows
LLM Website — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...
↓
Letigo Content Script — Intercepts streaming text via DOM
↓
Moss Semantic Engine (WASM · Local) — Validates against your rules
↓
Visual Feedback — Red strikethrough + compliance score
✓ Zero network calls · Everything stays in your browser

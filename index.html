How It Works

Three steps. Total control.

Set it once. Letigo silently enforces your rules on every AI chat you use, forever.

1

Install & set your rules

Add the extension, click the icon, and define your rules once. "Keep answers under 50 words." "Never mention competitors." "Always use bullet points."

2

Chat normally

Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — any LLM. Type your questions like you always do. Letigo works invisibly in the background.

3

See violations caught live

Watch as the AI types. Rule-breaking text gets red strikethrough in real-time. A compliance score tells you exactly how trustworthy each response is.

Features

More than just better prompting.

Prompts get forgotten by turn 3. Letigo enforces forever.

Real-time enforcement

Catches violations as the AI types, word by word. Sub-10ms semantic validation means zero perceptible lag.

100% local processing

Moss runs as WASM in your browser. Your rules and chat data never leave your device. Open dev tools — zero network calls.

Semantic, not just keywords

Moss understands meaning. If your rule says "never mention Apple," it catches "the company from Cupertino" too.

Works across every LLM

One extension. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity — any LLM with a web interface. Universal enforcement.

Compliance scoring

Every response gets a 0-100% trust score. Know at a glance whether the AI stayed within your boundaries.

Set and forget

Define rules once in the popup. Enforcement persists across conversations, tabs, and browser restarts. No repeating yourself.

Technology

Powered by Moss.
Built for trust.

Letigo uses Moss — a sub-10ms semantic search engine running entirely in your browser as WebAssembly. At under 20kB, it's lightweight enough to validate every chunk of streaming AI output without you ever noticing it's there.
